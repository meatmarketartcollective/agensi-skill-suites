## An hour with the kit

1. Collect your bids (the contractor emails, the PDFs, the napkin quote).
2. Run the **Bid Comparison Matrix**. You get a normalized table: who's pricing what, what's missing from each bid, and where the "cheap" bid is actually the expensive one once scope is equalized.
3. Shortlist two. Verify both with the **License & Insurance Verification Checklist** before anyone sets foot on your property.
4. Call the references with the **Reference Call Script & Scorecard**. The script asks about the job after the job: cleanup, surprise invoices, how they handled the one thing that went wrong.
5. Before signing, run the **Contract Red Flag Checklist** on the winner's contract. Anything it flags, you ask about in writing.

## Why a suite

Bid comparison alone doesn't tell you if the low bidder is licensed. A red-flag checklist alone doesn't help if you never checked references. This is one job with five failure points, and the suite closes each one, in order, in about an hour of your time.
